Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

Aves Belissimas Studio ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @avesbelissimas ("the operator"). The app is used only by the operator.

2. Data the app handles

  • TikTok authorization tokens of the operator's own account, created when the operator signs in with TikTok. They let the app upload and publish videos.
  • Basic profile information of the same account (for example the display name and avatar), read with the user.info.basic scope.
  • Videos that the operator chooses to upload.

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users and does not track visitors of this website. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the operator's videos to the operator's TikTok account. It is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

4. Storage and service providers

Authorization tokens are stored by the authentication service the app uses to connect to TikTok. Videos are stored on the operator's own computer. TikTok processes the uploaded videos under the TikTok Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

The operator can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions). After revocation the tokens stop working and are deleted from the app. To ask for deletion of any other data, write to the contact below.

6. Children

The app is not for children. It is not offered to the public.

7. Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest update.
