Aves Belissimas Studio

Aves Belissimas Studio is a small personal tool. The owner of the TikTok account @avesbelissimas uses it to prepare original nature videos and upload them to that same account.

What the app does

  • Signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) so the account owner can authorize the app.
  • Reads basic profile information of the authorized account (user.info.basic).
  • Uploads and publishes videos that the account owner chose (video.upload, video.publish) with the Content Posting API.

Who uses it

Only the account owner. The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other TikTok users.

Content labeling

Videos that are generated or edited with AI are labeled as AI-generated content when they are posted.
